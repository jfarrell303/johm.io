# Meshi Mania

<iframe src="../meshi_mania/index.html" width="512" height="512" style="border:none; overflow:hidden;" scrolling="no"></iframe>
!!! info

    A game made in about 96hs during the 2026 GMTK game jam!
    You are counting down the cost of the buffet until you can make a profit with a limited amount of room in your stomach. Can you beat the entrance fee of the buffet before you have no room left? The claw is also on a count down.

> Go to [itch.io page](https://jfarrell303.itch.io/meshimania)
